Key Findings: Spice Route Kitchens Operations Analytics

1. Overall Compliance

* Average compliance score: 82.71%.
* Target compliance threshold: 80%.
* Compliance-bearing submissions meeting their applicable threshold: 1,763 out of 2,899 (60.81%).

2. Daily Checklist Completion

* Outlets analyzed: 20.
* Analysis period: 91 calendar days.
* Expected opening and closing checklist submissions: 3,640.
* Actual daily checklist submissions: 2,719.
* Missing daily checklist submissions: 921.
* Daily checklist completion rate: 74.70%.

3. Outlets Requiring Attention

The following outlets had particularly low compliance scores:

* Delhi - Connaught Place: 54.83%.
* Ahmedabad - SG Highway: 57.57%.
* Nagpur - Civil Lines: 62.63%.

These outlets should be prioritized for follow-up and corrective action.

4. Ticket Analysis

* Total tickets: 304.
* Closed tickets: 221.
* Escalated tickets: 42.
* Open tickets: 41.
* High-priority tickets: 62.

Recurring issue themes included equipment failures, facility maintenance, hygiene, safety, staffing shortages, and stock shortages.

5. Recommended Actions

1. Improve opening and closing checklist completion.
2. Prioritize outlets with low compliance.
3. Review open, escalated, and overdue tickets.
4. Investigate recurring safety, hygiene, and equipment issues.
5. Review performance trends with leadership weekly.

Methodology Notes

* The expected daily checklist count is calculated as 20 outlets × 91 days × 2 daily checklists.
* Missing submissions are calculated as expected daily checklist submissions minus actual daily checklist submissions.
* The 80% compliance target is based on the assignment dataset.
* The 60.81% pass rate applies to compliance-bearing submissions, not all form submissions.
