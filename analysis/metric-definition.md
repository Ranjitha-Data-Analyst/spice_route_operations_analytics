KPI Metric Definitions

1. Average Compliance (%)

Definition: Average compliance score across submissions that contain a compliance percentage.

Calculation: Average of the compliance_pct field.

Result: 82.71%.

2. Compliance Pass Rate (%)

Definition: Percentage of compliance-bearing submissions that meet their applicable threshold.

Calculation: (Submissions meeting threshold / Total compliance-bearing submissions) × 100.

Result: (1,763 / 2,899) × 100 = 60.81%.

3. Expected Daily Checklist Submissions

Definition: Expected number of opening and closing checklist submissions across all outlets during the analysis period.

Calculation: Number of outlets × Calendar days × Daily checklist types.

Result: 20 × 91 × 2 = 3,640.

4. Missing Daily Checklist Submissions

Definition: Expected daily checklist submissions that were not recorded.

Calculation: Expected submissions − Actual daily checklist submissions.

Result: 3,640 − 2,719 = 921.

5. Daily Checklist Completion Rate (%)

Definition: Percentage of expected daily checklist submissions that were recorded.

Calculation: (Actual daily checklist submissions / Expected daily checklist submissions) × 100.

Result: (2,719 / 3,640) × 100 = 74.70%.

6. Ticket Metrics

* Total tickets: Count of all ticket records.
* Open tickets: Tickets with Open status.
* Escalated tickets: Tickets with Escalated status.
* Closed tickets: Tickets with Closed status.
* High-priority tickets: Tickets marked High priority.

Important Notes

* The analysis covers 91 calendar days.
* Expected submissions include daily opening and closing checklists only.
* Ad hoc forms are excluded from the missing daily checklist calculation.
* The compliance pass rate and average compliance percentage measure different things.
