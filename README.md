**Spice Route Kitchens | Operations Analytics**

Interactive Tableau Dashboards & Weekly Operations Reporting

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes three months of operational data from Spice Route Kitchens, a 20-outlet restaurant chain, to identify operational performance gaps, monitor checklist compliance, and track issue resolution.

The solution includes an interactive Tableau dashboard for Area Managers and a weekly operations report designed for leadership.

🎯 Business Objectives

* Monitor daily opening and closing checklist completion.
* Track operational compliance against an 80% target.
* Identify underperforming outlets.
* Monitor ticket status, priority, categories, and overdue issues.
* Provide leadership with recurring operational trends and actionable recommendations.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Tableau: Interactive dashboards, KPI visualizations, filters, and drill-down analysis.
* Data Analysis: Data aggregation, trend analysis, and performance comparisons.
* Business Intelligence: Operational KPI monitoring and management reporting.

📊 Key Findings

Daily checklist completion rate	74.70%
Tickets recorded	304
High-priority tickets	62

🔎 Key Insights

* Overall average compliance was 82.71%, slightly above the 80% target.
* Only 60.81% of compliance-bearing submissions met their applicable threshold.
* 921 daily checklist submissions were missing, indicating a need to improve operational follow-through.
* Delhi - Connaught Place, Ahmedabad - SG Highway, and Nagpur - Civil Lines were among the lowest-performing outlets for compliance.
* Ticket analysis highlighted recurring equipment, facilities, hygiene, safety, staffing, and stock issues.

💡 Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize corrective action at low-compliance outlets.
2. Improve daily opening and closing checklist completion.
3. Review overdue and high-priority tickets regularly.
4. Investigate recurring equipment, safety, and hygiene issues.
5. Use weekly performance reviews to track corrective actions and operational trends.
