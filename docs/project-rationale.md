Project Rationale: Spice Route Kitchens Operations Analytics

1. Business Context

Spice Route Kitchens operates 20 restaurant outlets. The analysis covers three months of operational data, including daily checklists, audits, and issue-tracking tickets.

The objective was to provide operational teams with timely performance visibility and help leadership identify recurring issues and improvement opportunities.

2. Dashboard Design Decisions

Area Manager Dashboard

The dashboard focuses on daily operational monitoring. Key metrics include checklist submissions, compliance, missing submissions, and ticket status, priority, and categories.

Outlet-level filtering enables Area Managers to identify underperforming locations and prioritize corrective actions.

Weekly Leadership Report

The leadership report focuses on broader trends, outlet comparisons, overdue issues, and recurring operational problems. A weekly review cadence supports performance monitoring and follow-up on corrective actions.

3. KPI Prioritization

The dashboards prioritize metrics that support operational decisions:

* Compliance against the 80% target.
* Daily checklist completion and missing submissions.
* Outlet-level performance.
* Ticket status, priority, and overdue issues.

Detailed question-level answers and free-text responses are better suited to focused investigation than the main dashboard.

4. Tool Selection

Tableau was selected to present operational metrics through interactive dashboards, filters, trend charts, and outlet comparisons. It allows users to explore performance without relying exclusively on static reports.

5. Reporting Format and Cadence

The Area Manager dashboard supports ongoing operational monitoring. The leadership report is designed for weekly review and can be shared as a PDF alongside the interactive dashboard link.

Automated scheduled report delivery has not been implemented.

6. Key Recommendations

1. Improve daily checklist completion across outlets.
2. Prioritize corrective action at low-compliance outlets.
3. Review high-priority and overdue tickets.
4. Investigate recurring equipment, hygiene, and safety issues.
5. Track corrective actions during weekly leadership reviews.

Conclusion

The solution separates immediate operational monitoring from leadership-level trend analysis. This approach helps different audiences focus on the metrics and actions most relevant to their responsibilities.
